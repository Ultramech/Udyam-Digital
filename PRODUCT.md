# Product

## What it is
Udyam Digital: the app a small-business owner in India uses to find the right business bundle from Airtel Business (internet, SIMs, WhatsApp/SMS, cloud email, security, CCTV and POS connectivity), buy it, complete KYC and e-sign on a phone, and follow the installation until it is live. A back-office console serves Airtel's sales, partners and operations.

## Users and their situation
- **Owner (primary).** Runs a kirana, clinic, salon, CA firm, coaching centre, restaurant or small factory. Uses a mid-range Android phone in a shop, often one-handed, often between customers. Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or English. Distrusts product codes and hidden charges. Wants one monthly price and to be told what happens next.
- **Decision-maker who is not the owner.** Receives a shared plan link and has to decide without logging in.
- **Staff (secondary).** Sales reps, channel partners and operations use the console on a laptop to follow leads, review KYC and move orders along.

## Mode
**Operate** for the customer app and the console: scanability, one clear next action, native-app expectations. **Persuade** only for the signed-out welcome screen.

## Product truths the design must carry
- One monthly price, with GST shown, never product codes.
- The rules engine decides eligibility and price; the AI explains. Every plan says what is included and what is not and why.
- Consent comes before KYC. Identifiers are masked. Documents are not stored.
- Everything is real: SMS codes, payments (test mode), e-sign, fibre check, installation booking. Nothing pretends.
- The owner should never need to call: status, next step and a person are always one tap away.

## Principles
1. **One screen, one job, one primary action.**
2. **The next step is always visible.** Home answers "what should I do now?" before anything else.
3. **Plain words in the owner's language.** No jargon, no codes, no all-caps labels.
4. **Honest states.** Pending, failed, waiting on us, waiting on you: each is named and has a way forward.
5. **Calm, not busy.** This is a purchase made with real money: surfaces are quiet, numbers are large and clear, colour means something.

## Anti-references
Not a clone of Airtel Thanks: no wall of banners, no red-everywhere chrome, no offer carousels. Not a generic SaaS dashboard: no purple gradients, no stat-card grids with invented numbers, no icon-in-circle feature rows.
